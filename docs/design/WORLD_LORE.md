# World Lore — Guarem (E02)

## Overview
Guarem is a world protected by divine beings called **Bảo Hộ** (Guardians). After *The Disappearance of Danvial*, void rifts opened and monsters flooded the land. Seven **Ma Thần** (Demon Gods) waged war until the Guardian of Destruction used forbidden magic to seal them.

## Timeline
1. **Age of Balance** — Guardians protect civilizations.
2. **The Disappearance of Danvial** — forbidden contact with void; rifts open.
3. **War of Seven Demons** — Demon Gods invade; Guardians unite.
4. **Great Sealing** — Demon Gods imprisoned; peace returns.
5. **Seal Fracture (present era)** — rifts reopen; protagonist begins journey at age 17.
