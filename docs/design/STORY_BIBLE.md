# Story Bible (E03)

## Protagonist
- 17-year-old orphan raised by a senior monk after a childhood tragedy.
- Goal: discover origin and role in the re-emerging void crisis.
- MVP name placeholder: **Ael**.

## Main Story Arc (MVP)
1. **Awakening** — arrive at Creation City outskirts
2. **Investigation** — learn about temple relic and void cult activity
3. **Confrontation** — fight Void Guardian mini-boss at ruins
4. **Choice** — trust Temple (Branch A) or pursue forbidden truth (Branch B)
5. **Ending** — 2 endings based on branch

## Branch A — Path of Light
- Ally with Temple of Creation
- Ending: `ending_guardian_pact` — become sworn protector of the relic

## Branch B — Path of Truth
- Investigate Guardian of Destruction's disappearance
- Ending: `ending_void_whisper` — uncover partial truth, cliffhanger for Phase 2

## Side Quests (MVP)
- `side_collect_herbs` — gather 3 herbs in forest region
- `side_talk_mercenary` — AI NPC conversation with affinity reward
